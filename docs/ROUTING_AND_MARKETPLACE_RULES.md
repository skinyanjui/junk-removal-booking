# Routing and Marketplace Rules

## Core principle

The platform routes an opportunity. It does not assign a crew before a provider submits a quote and the customer selects it.

## Eligibility

A provider is eligible when:

- The customer ZIP is active in provider coverage.
- The provider accepts the service and item type.
- Availability overlaps the requested timing.
- Required capabilities are present.
- Verification meets marketplace policy.
- The opportunity meets the provider minimum value.

## Ranking

Rank eligible providers using distance, response history, completion rate, rating, relevant capability, availability, and fair lead distribution. Ranking should not become pay-to-win.

## Routing wave

1. Notify the strongest five matches.
2. Observe provider review and quote activity.
3. Expand to the next group only when necessary.
4. Stop when booked, cancelled, or expired.

## Customer quote view

Default to Best match and show only three offers initially. Allow Lowest price, Soonest pickup, and Highest rated sorting. High-value filters are Available today, Insurance verified, and Dismantling included.

## Price control

A provider cannot silently increase the total. Any increase must include a reason and customer approval before additional work begins.
